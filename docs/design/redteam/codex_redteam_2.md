**Verdict: BUILD WITH AMENDMENTS.** The revision is much closer to buildable, but the range rule still has a round-one blocker, and the undo and research rules introduce new implementation blockers. Amend those before their build phases.

1. **BLOCKER - [GDD 9.3](/C:/Dev/FoulFowl/docs/design/GDD.md:488):** Equal-speed Close versus Stand Off holds the starting distance **10 indefinitely**: `-s + s = 0`. From 12, it settles at 11. Beak fleets cannot reach their 0-3 band in a common pairing. **Amend:** give closing a defined edge over an equally fast opener, then calculate the full posture/speed table for P6.

2. **MAJOR - [GDD 9.2-9.4](/C:/Dev/FoulFowl/docs/design/GDD.md:479):** The five orders offer useful agency, but "the only mid-turn prompt" can still mean several cards in one turn, while the time budget allows about 25 cards *per game*. H5 measures clicks, not whether orders matter. **Amend:** batch simultaneous battles into one decision stop, define which plan wins when friendly fleets combine, and test whether changing orders alters losses or outcomes.

3. **MAJOR - [GDD 5.2-5.5](/C:/Dev/FoulFowl/docs/design/GDD.md:220):** Wide play looks viable, perhaps still too automatic. At 7/12 pop, the printed growth formula replaces a drained pop in roughly **three turns**, not the stated eight; 1 credit upkeep is small, and 30% habitable orbits still yield about a 66% chance of a habitable world in a three-orbit system. The distance penalty matters chiefly beyond 15 pc. **Amend:** correct the pop estimate and use H3 plus per-colony payback data before raising costs.

4. **MAJOR - [GDD 12.4, 17.2](/C:/Dev/FoulFowl/docs/design/GDD.md:599):** Forced war creates a hunt, but a 25-turn attack followed by 15 turns of cooldown can repeat without producing an ending. A five-point leader-power drop does not establish that the Coalition prevents a cap finish. **Amend:** report repeated trips, captures, capitulations, and Called Games after a trip; tune the duration or targeting from those results.

5. **MAJOR - [GDD 12.3](/C:/Dev/FoulFowl/docs/design/GDD.md:590):** Grand Roost is reachable with a strong ally, but the stated solo arithmetic is incomplete: 52% own votes plus 15% pledged clears two-thirds, **yet an AI refuses the decisive pledge to a non-ally**. Without allied or voluntary support, a candidate needs two-thirds on its own. **Amend:** specify how unpledged AI votes are chosen and state the actual allied and unallied paths to victory.

6. **BLOCKER - [GDD 6.1-6.2](/C:/Dev/FoulFowl/docs/design/GDD.md:314):** The new "two further tiers" rule can never unlock rejected **T5 or T6** options through research: there is no T7 or T8. Creative therefore gets both options in all 36 nodes while ordinary empires permanently lose 12 late options across six fields; its eight-pick price has no supporting balance gate. **Amend:** explicitly make those forks permanent or define a final-tier unlock, then measure Owl results before setting `creative_tier_cost_pct`.

7. **MAJOR - [GDD 17.3; Architecture 7.3](/C:/Dev/FoulFowl/docs/design/GDD.md:725):** P1's "2x economy and no bonuses" is ambiguous, and 8/10 wins against a passive capital mainly proves that overwhelming force can finish a siege. **Amend:** define 2x as starting assets or production, keep that smoke probe, and add a parity-economy probe against an opponent that reinforces. Keep AI planning after End Turn; player-turn precomputation is optional and must be discarded when orders or knowledge change.

8. **BLOCKER - [Architecture 4.5](/C:/Dev/FoulFowl/docs/design/ARCHITECTURE.md:122):** Hand-written `revert` for every growing command kind is brittle for the proposed builder. More concretely, undo restores state while the command log still contains "every applied command," so replay can diverge. **Amend:** use a shared field-change recorder with reverse-order restoration; specify how undo removes or records the command in the replay log, and test apply-undo-redo-replay equality.

9. **MAJOR - [GDD 17.2](/C:/Dev/FoulFowl/docs/design/GDD.md:708):** The new soak arithmetic is correct: 70 subsets x 4 = 280 games; each race appears 140 times, each pair 60; conditional win rates sum to 200%, so 40% Swans leaves 22.9% on average for each other race. But a Wilson interval merely overlapping 35-45% is a loose gate for the declared 40% ñ5 target. **Amend:** report point estimates against that target and use intervals to express uncertainty, rather than treating overlap as confirmation.
