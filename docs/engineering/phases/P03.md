## Goal
Ships exist as designs on hulls; colonies build them; fleets move by `ceil(distance / speed)` inside fuel range, through the wormhole in one turn; Nest Ships found colonies (draining a pop from the builder), Stake Ships plant outposts; each empire sees only what its scanners cover; scouts auto-explore. The player designs ships and orders fleets with the mouse.

## Playable at the end
Start a game: two Glances, a Nest Ship and two Sparrows sit at home. Toggle auto-explore on the Glances and watch the fog lift; send the Nest Ship to a good world and colonise it; queue a second Nest Ship (the capital loses a pop when it completes); build a Stake Ship and plant an outpost on an asteroid to extend the range overlay; design a Kestrel in the designer. AI empires expand only through scenario helpers (their planners arrive in P05).

static func advance(gs, fleet: Fleet) -> void                         # integer lerp between from and dest by elapsed/total turns

**Design legality** (refusal keys): exactly one drive (`refuse.design_drive`), at most one plate/mantle/computer (`refuse.design_slot`), specials count ≤ hull specials (`refuse.design_specials`), space used ≤ hull space (`refuse.design_space`), every part's tech known (`refuse.tech_unknown`), mounts only on Talon weapons (`refuse.design_mount`), `loot_only` parts only for the looting empire (`refuse.design_loot`), at most `max_designs` non-obsolete designs (`refuse.design_limit`). Space: `pct` parts use `ceil_div(hull_space x pct, 100)`; flat parts multiply by `mini_pct` then the mount's `space_pct`, rounding up each time. PP likewise from `pp`/`pp_pct` of hull PP; plate PP = `pct(hull pp, plate pp_pct)`. HP = `pct(hull hp, plate hp_pct)` + special `hp_pct` sums. Upkeep = hull upkeep if armed, else 0. Armed = any weapon or bomb part.

**Starting designs and fleet** (`StartState`, per empire, GDD §4.4): designs "Glance" (small, walk_drive, glance_pod), "Nest Ship" (medium, walk_drive, nest_pod), "Sparrow" (small, walk_drive, 4 x wick_talon). One fleet at the homeworld with 2 Glance, 1 Nest Ship; a second fleet with 2 Sparrows. Check: Sparrow space 4 (walk 15% of 24, ceil) + 20 = 24; Nest Ship 65 PP.

**Production of ships**: `QueueItem.kind == "ship"`, `ref_id` = design id; buildable only at colonies with a building that `counts_as_yard` (the_yard or grand_nest); `yard_only` hulls only at the capital; cost `DesignRules.stats().pp` x (100 + ship_cost_pct)/100. A completed ship joins an idle own fleet at that system with no order (lowest id), else a new fleet. **Nest Pod drain**: a design with `colonize` completes only if the colony has ≥ `nest_min_pop_units` pop units; then it loses 1000 milli; otherwise the item waits complete-but-unlaunched (progress kept, a `notify.nest_waiting` notice once). **Colony Base**: queue kind `colony_base`, `ref_id` = target planet id (same system, habitable for the species, unowned), cost `colony_base_pp`, drains 1 pop the same way, founds the colony at completion.

**Movement step** (ARCHITECTURE step 2): fleets in transit advance; arrivals set `system_id`, clear `dest_system_id`; explored by arrival.
**Orbital step** (step 4; colonisation/outposts now, siege in P04): each fleet with order colonize/outpost at its target's system: validate again; conflicts on one planet in the same turn → keyed coin `Rng.keyed(seed, turn, Rng.COLONIZE, planet_id)` picks the winner among claimants sorted by empire id (P04 inserts "orbit controller wins" first); the winner's colony ship is consumed: colony with `new_colony_pop_milli` (Spare Nest special: 2000), preset frontier, `founded_turn`; outposts are `Colony` objects with `is_outpost = true`, pop 0, no jobs, no queue. Notices `notify.colony_founded` (GROK1 B7).
**Finance**: ship upkeep = Σ armed ships' hull upkeep x (100 + ship_upkeep_pct)/100 (ceil).
**Visibility step** (step 13): scan circles: colonies `colony_scan_dpc` (+`capital_scan_add_dpc`, + `scan_add`), outposts `ship_scan_dpc`, ships `ship_scan_dpc` (+ glance pods); systems inside any circle become explored; colonies and fleets inside are seen (buildings only within colony scan); an empire is met when any of its colonies or fleets is seen; known designs are not revealed by sight (P04 battles reveal them).
**Governor step** additions: fleets with `auto_explore` and no destination pick the nearest unexplored, in-range star not already targeted by another own auto-exploring fleet (ties lowest id) and move there; none → stay.
