## Goal
Battles happen wherever parties at war meet (empires, colony defenses, monsters). Before resolution each side fixes orders: the AI from what it sees, the player on **one Battle Orders stop per turn** (≤ 3 cards, the rest standing plans). A deterministic, order-independent resolver produces a `BattleLog`; the viewer replays it and never changes it. After combat the orbit controller blockades, bombards, razes outposts and invades. The Guardian of Orn and two Leviathans guard the map.

## Playable at the end
A scripted war (a scenario action or the debug "declare war" button on the Diplomacy stub in the top-bar menu, removed in P05) lets you fight: a Battle Orders card shows odds, the five orders and the projection line ("Talon band by round 3, Beak band by round 7 at worst"); the viewer shows the range strip with the band words, ships as bird silhouettes, darts being swatted, sparks and feathers; the autopsy names the deciding band; you blockade a colony, bomb it with Bomb Bays, and invade it with Boot Ships. Poking the Guardian shows its warning card and marks you if you run.

## Rules (code-level; GDD wins on any doubt)
### War state (`Wars.gd`, static; P05 adds commands, P08 relations)
`is_at_war(gs, a, b) -> bool` (monster faction 100 is at war with everyone), `set_war(gs, a, b, on: bool)`. Stored as `gs.wars["<min>_<max>"] = true`.

### Building a battle (`StepCombat` part a, then b)
1. Contested systems: any system with ≥ 2 parties mutually at war where at least one has an armed ship or an armed colony defense. Parties: per empire, all its fleets at the system (in-system only) form one party; a colony's defenses form a separate party of the colony owner (planet, `s = 0`, never in the line count). One battle per system.
2. **Orders**: for each party: AI and monsters → `CombatBuilder.ai_orders(...)` (P04: the fleet's standing plan; P05 replaces with `AiBattle`). Player parties → standing plan of the fleet with the largest armed PP (ties lowest fleet id); line order = concatenation of the fleets' line orders in that ranking, then default order for ships not listed (Swat Escorts and ships of the posture's band first, then other warships by hull space descending, unarmed last; ties ship id).
3. **The stop**: a player battle qualifies when `orders_mode == "always"`, or `orders_mode == "big"` and the player has ≥ 1 armed ship there and (own armed ships ≥ `big_battle_armed` or enemy armed ships ≥ `big_battle_armed` or any bomb part on either side). Qualifying battles sorted by total armed PP (both sides, desc; ties system id); the first `orders_stop_cards` become requests; the rest are listed as `auto`. If any request exists: `TurnProcessor` returns `NEEDS_INPUT` with `requests: Array[Dictionary]` (`{system_id, enemy_visible: {...}, odds_pct, plan: {...}, projection: {talon_round, beak_round}}` and `auto: [system ids]`). `orders_mode == "never"` or headless (`run_all`) → standing plans. Answers are `CmdBattleOrders` (`system_id`, posture, target_priority, swat_mode, retreat_threshold, line_order), validated and appended to `gs.cmd_log` with source `battle`. They never enter the undo list.
4. **Resolve** (part b): per contested system ascending id: `CombatBuilder.build(gs, db, system_id, orders) -> CombatInput` → `CombatResolver.resolve(input) -> BattleLog` → `CombatApply.apply(gs, db, log)`.

### `RangeTrack`
```gdscript
class_name RangeTrack  # static; GDD §9.3 verbatim; must match range_reference.py on all 7,488 fixture rows
static func step(d: int, p_a: int, s_a: int, p_b: int, s_b: int) -> int
static func project(d0: int, p_own: int, s_own: int, s_enemy: int) -> Dictionary   # {"talon_round": int, "beak_round": int}, worst case (enemy P 12)
static func preferred(posture: String, auto_band: String, has_horizon_ammo: bool) -> int   # close 0, talon 5, standoff 12, retreat 12, auto: beak 1 / talon 5 / horizon 12 (horizon falls back to the next band when ammo is spent)
```
Start distance per pair: `stalemates[system]` carry-over value if the same two empires fought there last turn and stalemated; else `start_distance_two_steps` when the defender at its own colony system has the `two_steps_back_home` flag; else `start_distance`. Party speed `s` = min combat speed (design combat speed + `combat_speed_add` + lean_in) over its armed **line** ships, at least 1; planets 0; monsters 1.

### `CombatResolver.resolve(input: CombatInput) -> BattleLog` (GDD §9.5 order, per round 1..`battle_rounds`)
1. Reserves fill empty line slots in line order (Titan takes 2 slots; party capacity `line_slots` from races.json, +1 with `line_slots_plus_1`); then each pair's `D = RangeTrack.step(...)` (retreating parties use P 12).
2. Horizon in flight arrives at its target: in Missiles-first mode each live Swat mount in the target's party takes `swat_shots` shots at `clamp(hit_base + acc, hit_min, hit_max)` (acc = computer + traits + mount +20 + veterancy), one missile removed per hit; surviving missiles roll `clamp(missile_hit_base + missile acc − miss_field − evasion/2 − scatter_molt 20, hit_min, hit_max)`; damage = fixed x (100 + band pct)/100 − shield, min 0.
3. All line mounts fire (Gone to Molt ships fire; they cannot be targeted in rounds 1-2; Swat mounts that intercepted this round do not also fire; in Ships-first mode Swat mounts fire here at ships and never intercept). Per shot: target by `Targeting`, to-hit `clamp(hit_base + acc − evasion − (beak ? beak_acc_per_distance x D : 0), hit_min, hit_max)`; damage `range_i(min, max)` x (100 + `band_damage_pct` + v_formation + flush round 1)/100; Talon x (100 − falloff x D)/100; minus shield (Talon full, Beak `shield/2` floored or reduced by `beak_ignores_shields_*`), min 0. **All shots of the step are computed against the state at the start of the step and applied together.** `Not This Feather`: the shot misses outright on a keyed 25% roll before the to-hit roll.
4. Horizon launchers with ammo launch (a missile object per launcher: target chosen now, arrives next round; `salvos` decrements; Indoor Torpedo fires on even rounds only, unlimited).
5. Cleanup: hp ≤ 0 → destroyed; Self-Sealing heals 10% of max (not above max); retreat checks (`Retreat`); end early when at most one mutually hostile **armed** side remains.
After the last round with several armed hostile sides: stalemate (store each pair's D in `stalemates`, no orbit control).

**RNG**: every random draw uses its own keyed generator: `Rng.keyed(seed, turn, Rng.COMBAT, system_id, Rng.hash_ints([round, step, empire_id, unit_uid, mount_index, shot_index]))`. With this, shuffling the input arrays must not change the log (order-independence test).

**Targeting** (`Targeting.pick(shooter_unit, mount, candidates, priority, D) -> int uid`): candidates = enemy line units that are targetable this round (planets are targetable; a defense party at 0 HP is suppressed and not targetable). Key 1 = the priority (`biggest`: hull space desc; `swat`: has a Swat mount; `band_*`: the unit's main band equals it; `defenses`: planet units; `transports`: unarmed units with pods; `auto`: no key). Key 2 = Auto score: prefer units whose remaining HP ≤ this mount's max damage after shield (can die now), then the lowest remaining HP among units this mount can damage at all. Key 3 = lowest uid.

### Retreat (`Retreat.gd`, GDD §9.6)
Retreat threshold is evaluated at battle start with `CombatMath.odds_pct(own, enemy)` (`strength = isqrt(total_hp x expected_damage_per_round_vs_their_average_shield)`): `half` → retreat if odds < `odds_half_pct`, `even` → if < `odds_even_pct`. A retreating party escapes at the end of round `R`: base `retreat_round` (2); own `instant_regret` → 1; enemy `enemy_retreat_plus_2` → +2; enemy `flush` trait in the first battle of a war → +1; enemy `no_exit_home` at the enemy's colony system → 8. While retreating it moves toward 12 and fires only Swat mounts. On escape exactly one **receipt**: the slowest ship (ties: most damaged, then lowest id) is destroyed; then the fleet(s) head for the nearest own colony in range (set destination). Guardian-Marked: any party that fought the Guardian and retreated gets a `TimedMod` `band_damage_pct` −10 (all bands) for 10 turns.

### After the battle (`CombatApply`)
Losses applied to ships/defenses (defense HP to 0 = suppressed); veterancy +`veterancy_step` (max 15) for surviving ships of the side holding the field; the sole remaining armed side controls orbit: enemy unarmed ships there are destroyed; the winner's empire learns the loser's designs it fought (Knowledge.known_designs). Guardian killed → `gs.loot = {"guardian": empire_id}` (Orn-Plating legal for that empire; 2 techs granted in P06: record `pending_guardian_techs: 2`), Orn colonisable. Report entries: `military.battle` with system, sides, result, autopsy line, and the log index for Replay.

### Siege (`StepOrbital`, after combat; in this order per system ascending id)
1. **Razing**: an outpost whose system has a hostile armed fleet controlling orbit at end of turn is removed (`notify.outpost_razed`, GAP key).
2. **Blockade**: a colony with a hostile armed fleet controlling orbit gets `blockaded = true` (no growth, industry −50% via Modifiers, no trade income from P08); else false (`notify.blockaded` / `notify.blockade_lifted`, GAP keys).
3. **Bombardment**: fleets with order `bombard` controlling orbit: milli = Σ bomb parts' `bomb_milli` (doubled by `toxic_preening`), then x (100 + best mantle `bomb_pct`)/100 except `bomb_ignores_shield` parts, which add their 2000 after. Pop never below 0; a colony reaching 0 pop is destroyed (planet free). Guns never kill pop. Record `bombarded_by` on the colony for P08 diplomacy.
4. **Invasion**: fleets with order `invade` controlling orbit, target colony defenses suppressed or absent: attackers = 4 marines per Boot Pod at `(marine_base + marine_str_add) x (100 + troops_pct)/100`; defenders = garrison (first) at `marine_base + marine_str_add` and militia `ceil(pop_units/2)` at `(militia_base + militia_str_add) x (100 + troops_pct + ground_def_pct)/100`. Duels in order (attacker unit i vs current defender): `Rng.keyed(seed, turn, Rng.GROUND, colony_id, duel_index)`: attacker wins when `range_i(1, s_a + s_d) <= s_a`; loser removed. Win → owner changes, species/pop/buildings kept, queue cleared, `occupied_until = turn + occupied_turns` (unification attacker: none), Boot Ships consumed; capital captured → that empire's `capital_colony_id = -1`. Loss → Boot Ships consumed. Notices `notify.invasion_won/lost` (GROK1 B7).
5. Colonisation (P03 rule) with **the orbit controller winning** conflicts first.

### Colony defense party (`Defenses.gd`)
Built from buildings: Horizon Perch launchers (best known Horizon weapon, else the `fallback` profile; unlimited ammo), Talon Batteries (4 primary-mounted best Talons), Ugly Perch (6 best Talons, its own shield = best known mantle), defense HP pool and planet shield as in Data. Regeneration `defense_regen_pct` of max per turn (population step). Garrison regen +`garrison_regen` up to the barracks size.

## Traps
- PLAN §7 and ARCHITECTURE §17 items 18 and 22: orders fixed before `resolve`; `RangeTrack.step` verbatim.
- Never resolve shots sequentially against live HP: snapshot each step.
- One keyed RNG per draw; never a shared `Rng` across units.
- The viewer reads the log only; it never calls sim code that mutates state.
- No full rings for shields, selection or explosions (the explosion fireball may be a filled disc; shields are jagged arc segments).
- `BattleLog` must be serialisable (`to_dict`) for save and replay; keep only the last 20 logs in `gs.battle_logs`.

## Do NOT
- No AI battle decisions beyond standing plans (P05 `AiBattle`). No diplomacy penalties for bombardment (P08 reads `bombarded_by`). No tech capture or Guardian tech loot (P06). No line editor on the card (the fleet panel has it).
