## Goal
Every AI seat plays a complete game from its own fog-of-war knowledge through the same commands as the player: it expands, builds to a military budget, designs from its known parts, declares and ends wars, fights with counter-picked orders, invades, defends, and capitulates when hopeless. Games end by Conquest or Called Game. A soak tool runs many AI-only games headless; strategic probes test AI competence. **This is the first full evening: the owner plays it.**

## Playable at the end
Evening Standard as any of Swans, Pheasants, Ducks, Geese vs the three others: explore, expand, meet AIs (PNN announces contacts and wars), get attacked by the seated early-war AI, fight with Battle Orders, invade, accept a capitulation, and win by Conquest or reach the cap (Called Game) and see the victory/defeat screen with One More Turn. Research is not in this phase (everyone keeps the starting kit plus Guardian loot); P06 adds the tree.

## Rules
### Relations (P05 subset; P08 completes GDD §12.1)
`Relations.value(db, gs, a, b) -> ModResult`: base (`relations_base` effects of a's view: charismatic +20 etc. of **b's** traits as seen by a), border tension (`border_tension_per_pair` per pair of a/b colonies within `border_pair_dpc`, cap `border_tension_cap`), war `relation_base_war`. Clamp −100..100. Lines for the tooltip.

### War and peace
`declare_war` (refuses: not met `refuse.not_met`, already at war, a truce running `refuse.truce`, self, monsters); sets war, records `war_started_turn[pair]` (for flush "first battle of a war"), PNN `news.war` (GROK1 B7). `propose_peace` (refuses: not at war) creates a proposal resolved in `StepDiplomacy`: AI accepts if its `AiWar.wants_peace` (ratio < `peace_ratio_pct`, or `peace_quiet_turns` without battle, or a second front; Librarian refuses for 20 turns after being attacked); acceptance ends the war and starts a `truce_turns` truce. The player answers AI proposals with `answer_proposal` (accept/decline) on a card in the summary.

### Military budget and the governor (GDD §5.7)
`Empire.military_budget` ∈ peace/guarded/war (player: TopBar dropdown → `set_military_budget`; AI: `AiProduction`). Each governor pass, per empire: `target = pct(total industry, budget pct)`; `current` = Σ industry of yard colonies (counts_as_yard; Heron+ only at the capital until P07) whose queue head is a warship; while `current < target` and fleet upkeep ≤ `budget_upkeep_stop_pct` of income: add one warship item (role from `MilitaryBudget.next_role(view)`: Swat Escort if enemy Horizon was seen and own Swat share < 25%, else the role whose band beats the highest enemy shield seen (Beak at shield ≥ 4 when Beak parts are known, else Talon), with `AutoDesign` for that role, saved as a design if new) at the yard colony with the most industry not already heading a warship, `added_by = "governor"`, `why_key = "summary.why_queued.budget"`, `why_args = {"policy", "target", "current"}`. Never more warships than the budget; never queues on its own when `budget = peace` and current ≥ target. Players see "why queued" in the queue tooltip.

### AI architecture (ARCHITECTURE §8)
```gdscript
class_name AiView extends RefCounted
static func build(gs: GameState, db: ContentDB, empire_id: int) -> AiView
	# Own entities: full read access through accessor methods (own_colonies(), own_fleets(), own_designs(), treasury(), ...).
	# Foreign: ONLY from gs.knowledge[empire_id] (seen colonies with intel age, visible fleets, known designs, met empires),
	# plus geometry (systems, planets of explored systems), difficulty vision as written into Knowledge, and read-only db.
	# No method returns a foreign Colony/Fleet/Empire object; it returns Knowledge dictionaries.
func rng(salt: int) -> Rng    # Rng.keyed(seed, turn, Rng.AI, empire_id, salt)

class_name AiPlayer   # static
static func plan_economy(view: AiView) -> Array[Cmd]       # AiColonies, AiDesign, AiProduction (budget policy), research hook (P06)
static func war_substeps() -> Array[StringName]             # [&"assess", &"expansion", &"military", &"war", &"diplomacy", &"espionage"]
static func plan_war_substep(view: AiView, substep: StringName, memory: AiMemory) -> Array[Cmd]
class_name AiBattle   # static
static func choose_orders(view: AiView, request: Dictionary) -> Dictionary   # GDD §13.3 counter-pick table
```
- **Precompute** (ARCHITECTURE §7.3): `SimGame.precompute_step() -> bool` advances one AI's `plan_economy` per call during the player's turn (Session/TurnRunner idle slices); results in `ai_held[empire_id]`; `StepAiPlan` applies held commands (computing any missing) and then runs the war substeps, one empire-substep per `next()` call. Undo/redo/load clear `ai_held` (restart).
- **Command hygiene**: AI commands go through `SimGame.submit_ai(cmd) -> String`; a refusal logs `SimLog.warn("AI_REJECT", "<kind> <key>")` and is never applied; the soak fails on any.
- **Assess**: power estimates (`Power.fleet_power(units) = (Σ isqrt(expected_dps_vs_shield x hp))²`), enemy colony defense estimates inflated `intel_inflate_pct_per_5` per 5 turns since seen, threats per own colony (visible enemy fleet power within `defense_response_turns` of travel), posture Peace/Tension/War/Losing.
- **Colonies**: preset by role (capital → capital; minerals rich+ → industry; best farm planets when food < need → breadbasket; RP-heavy species planets → research; frontier otherwise); never Manual.
- **Expansion**: target score `max_pop x 10 + mineral rank x 8 + special 5 − eta_turns x 5` (Patient Rock: hostile worlds x2); ships in flight count against `min(targets in range, 2 + expansion / 3)`; queues Nest/Stake Ships via `queue_add` at the best yard, sends them with `fleet_move` + `colonize`/`outpost`.
- **Military** (competence features GDD §13.3, each pinned by a probe): roles Defend/Strike/Invade/Reserve; strikes launch at `own ≥ strike_ratio_pct x estimated defense` (Floor 130); re-scout targets whose intel is older than `intel_max_age` before striking; after a failed strike (the strike fleet retreated or died), the next strike on that target needs `strike_retry_ratio_pct` of the defense actually met, max `strike_max_failures` per target per `strike_failure_window` turns (`AiMemory`); Boot Ships follow one turn behind and invade the turn orbit is secured, sized `marines ≥ invade_ratio_pct x defenders`; a visible enemy strike within `defense_response_turns` of a colony triggers reinforcement or a defense building (Fortress-list item) within `defense_response_deadline` turns.
- **War**: declare when (relation < `war_relation_threshold` or aggression ≥ `war_aggression_threshold`) and own/target power ratio ≥ `war_ratio_pct` x difficulty `war_threshold_pct` / 100, a target is reachable, and the personality's rules allow; Nestling never declares on the player first. Budget rule `war_on_contact` switches budget to war at first contact with a rival whose colony is within `budget_switch_contact_dpc`.
- **AiBattle** counter-pick (GDD §13.3): enemy Horizon-heavy and own Swat mounts → Missiles first + Close; faster Beak fleet → Close; slower Talon fleet vs Beak → Talon range; odds below the retreat threshold → Retreat; own Horizon-heavy → target Swat Escorts first; else the standing plan. Uses only the request's visible enemy data.

### Victory, score, capitulation (`StepVictory`)
- Elimination: an empire with no colonies (outposts do not count) is eliminated; its ships are removed.
- **Capitulation**: an AI at war that has lost its capital (`capital_colony_id == -1`), holds ≤ `capitulate_max_colonies` colonies and has power < `capitulate_power_pct` (`capitulate_power_pct_floor` against a Floor enemy) of its strongest enemy's offers surrender to that enemy: AI-to-AI accepted automatically; to the player a `CapitulationCard` (GROK2 title/body) → `accept_capitulation` (everything transfers; the empire is eliminated) or decline. The player never auto-capitulates.
- **Conquest**: all rivals eliminated or capitulated → winner. **Called Game**: at `turn_cap`, the highest `Score.of(empire)` (GDD §14 formula with the `score_*` keys; ties lowest id). **Defeat**: the player has no colonies, or another empire wins. `game_over` stops the pipeline; One More Turn sets `one_more_turn` and disables further victory checks.

## Soak and probes (`tools/soak/`)

  - **P2** surprise defenses, **P3** stale intel, **P4** invasion logistics, **P5** defense response: pass rules as GDD §17.3, computed from `AiMemory` and the command log.
  - **P10** precompute cache: 20 seeds x 30 turns, economy commands computed at turn start then random valid player planning commands applied equal those computed at End Turn; hashes equal.

## Traps
- PLAN §7; ARCHITECTURE §17 items 18 and 21 (precompute reads only own entities + Knowledge).
- An AI that reads `GameState` directly is a fairness bug; tests cannot catch every leak, so planners take `AiView` only.
- Every AI loop over candidates sorts with a tie-break by id.
- Planners must be sliced: one empire-substep per `next()`; `AiMilitary` processes one fleet group per call if it exceeds the 8 ms budget.
- Soak subprocesses are foreground children of `run_soak.py`; the script waits for every one and fails if any dies.
