## Goal
The galaxy talks: relations with a full breakdown, treaties and exchanges the AI accepts or refuses with reasons, a Grand Roost that can crown a Supreme Bird by the three printed routes (or be Defied), a Coalition that hunts the leader with a coordinated strike pair and always ends (Cut Down or Held → Acknowledged), espionage with a pick-one-of-three steal, and the Exodus door. All four victory doors exist.

## Playable at the end
Sign NAPs and trade deals, buy a pledge, vote at the Grand Roost, watch the Tier List form against the Swans with PNN naming the target, join the Coalition, Defy an AI Supreme Bird and read the card naming the leaders now at war with you, steal a tech by picking one of three, start the Departure Roost and survive the backlash.

## Rules (GDD wins; this is the code shape)
- **Relations** (`Relations.value`): every GDD §12.1 line as a `ModResult` line; grudges and favours live in `relations_mem["a_b"]` with per-line values that decay `decay_per_turn` toward 0 each turn (The Neighbor: grudges every other turn); territorial empires lose `territorial_per_turn` per turn with each empire that has a colony within `territorial_dpc` and no treaty, floored at `territorial_floor` for that line.
- **Treaties** (`Treaties.can_propose(a, b, type) -> String`): thresholds by relation as seen by the receiver; Research never with a distrusted empire (`refuse.distrust`); a repulsive party may only make Peace or War (`refuse.repulsive`); territorial: NAP only after a battle won against them or a tribute paid (`refuse.territorial`). AI acceptance: `value + relation/2 + personality bias ≥ 0`, returning the reason key (`diplo.reason.<id>`, GROK1 B7). Trade income per side `min(pop_a, pop_b)/4`, ramping linearly over `trade_ramp_turns`, x (100 + trade_income_pct)/100, stops while either is blockaded at its capital. Breaking a treaty by declaring war = **backstab** (grudges), except Coalition duty.
- **Exchanges** (`Exchange`): tech for tech/credits, credit gifts (favours), tribute demands; a receiver gets at most one tech by trade or gift per `transfer_every_turns` (`refuse.transfer_limit`, copy `diplo.reason.transfer_limit` GAP); Exodus keys never (`diplo.reason.exodus_locked`). Techs arrive through `Research.receive`.
- **Grand Roost** (`Council`, `StepCouncil`): sessions on the preset's `council_first` turn and every `council_every` while ≥ 3 empires live and the player has met one; announced `council_announce_turns` ahead (PNN); **the first session is non-binding** (publishes the count). Votes = pop units. Candidates = the two empires with most votes; **electable** = met every living empire and own votes ≥ `council_electable_pct`% of all. Pledges (`pledge_buy`): price `pledge_price_per_vote x votes x (1 + pledge_share_mult x buyer share)` (integer math in per-mille), counted for one candidate up to `pledge_cap_pct`% of all votes (excess pledged votes become abstentions), breaking one costs `grudge_broken_pledge`. **No AI refuses a pledge for kingmaker reasons.** Unpledged AI votes follow GDD §12.3 order (self, never the active Coalition leader, Acknowledged, ally, higher relation ≥ 0, abstain; ties lower id). Charismatic: +`charisma_share_pct`% of its votes to the candidate it votes for, never itself. Winner: an electable candidate with ≥ 2/3 of all votes (cast or abstained). Player → Grand Roost victory. AI → the player chooses `council_accept` (defeat) or `council_defy` (the Council dissolves forever; every empire that voted for the winner declares war on the player; `DefyCard` lists them by leader name, `council.defy_card.title` GROK2).
- **Coalition** (`Coalition`, updated in `StepDiplomacy`): power `fleet PP/20 + 3 x colonies + pop/2` (`Power.coalition_power`); trip conditions, effects, **strike pair** (the two members whose capitals are nearest the target colony = leader's colony nearest the members' power-weighted centroid; if the player is one of them, the next-nearest AI; `AiMilitary` sizes on the pair's combined strike force ≥ `coalition_strike_ratio_pct`% of the estimated defense and both fleets set the same arrival turn by waiting at a staging system), **duty** (every AI member not allied with the leader goes to war; NAP/Trade with the leader cancelled without backstab), shared vision and range; **endings**: Cut Down (the leader lost a colony to anyone at war with it, or its power fell below trip − `coalition_cutdown_margin` points) → dissolve, `coalition_cooldown` turns before it can trip again on that empire; Held (`coalition_turns` without a Cut Down) → dissolve, AI members at war offer a truce the AI leader auto-accepts, the leader becomes **Acknowledged** (never tripped again; AI bandwagon votes). Labels Tier List (Swans) / Snowball; the first trip in a game posts the Tier List Leaks bulletin (the event record is formalised in P09). Players may `coalition_join` (gain the shared vision/range and the relation bonus).
- **Espionage** (`Espionage`, `StepEspionage`): one spy target and funding (Off/Low/Med/High = 0/3/6/12 credits per turn), one security level at the same prices; `intel += funding x (100 + spy_score)/100`; threshold `spy_threshold_base + spy_threshold_per_tech x min(lackable techs, cap)`; on reaching it, success at `clamp(spy_success_base + spy − security, min, max)` where security = `2 x security funding + spy trait + Security Nest 25 − 20 if democracy`; success → up to `spy_choices` lackable techs (never Exodus keys), keyed draw: the player picks next turn (`steal_choose`, `StealChoiceCard`), the AI takes the highest-cost one; failure → `spy_caught_pct`% keyed chance of being caught (`grudge_spy_caught`). Intel resets either way. Stolen techs count as *seen* for Cache (P09).
- **Exodus** (`Exodus`): Departure Roost (`project: true`, capital only) is buildable when both `exodus_key` techs are known and every field's `highest_tier ≥ exodus_min_tier`; queueing it is announced (`news.exodus` GROK1 B7): every AI −30 relation (`departure_announce_relation`) and its war planner may target the capital; completion = Exodus victory. AI pursues it when its research weight is top-2 and the requirements are within reach (`AiResearch` weight on the keys).
- **Bombardment diplomacy**: `bombarded_by` → victim `grudge_bombarded` per turn, every other met empire `grudge_bombarded_witness`, Toxic Preening adds `grudge_toxic_extra` for everyone who sees it.
- **Victory clock**: Grand Roost row (votes in hand, need, electability), Exodus row (keys, fields at T5+, Departure Roost PP left), with ETAs.
