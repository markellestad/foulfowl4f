## Rules
- **Trait wiring** (each with a test): `night_hours` (+`night_hours_pct`% research while at peace with every met empire; Modifiers line), `cache` (every `cache_every` turns, one tech from the empire's *seen* set (seen in a diplomacy screen offer, a battle autopsy, or stolen) at `cache_cost_pct`% of its tier cost becomes the project; never an Exodus key; via `Research.receive(..., "cache")` on completion), `scavengers` (side holding the field: `scavenge_credit_pct`% of destroyed enemy ships' PP as credits; per destroyed enemy design a keyed `scavenge_tech_pct`% chance to learn one of its part techs), `informants` (Knowledge shows met empires' treasury, treaties and designs), `stealthy_ships` (enemy scan radius against this empire's fleets halved), `omniscient` (all planets and fleets visible; not on any premade), `unification` (captured colonies skip occupation), `nothing_wasted` (rush price `nothing_wasted_mult_pct`% of remaining, never doubled), `lucky` (never the target of a negative random-pool event; the pool is empty in MVP, so implement the filter in `Events` and test it with a fixture event; Perch Review is a rating, not a random event, and still applies), `creative`/`uncreative` (already in P06: test with the Owl and Chicken cards), `piscivore` + `tolerant` + `heat_intolerant` on Penguins (P02 precedence: fish table; no tolerant food floor for piscivore; heat cap after the pop floor).
- **Seating** (Evening Standard, GDD §2.1): AI seats Swans (Seat the Swans), one early-war (geese, then pheasants), one schemer (crows, then ducks), refilled from the remaining races in id order when the player takes one; Tiny: Swans + one early-war. The New Game card prints `menu.preset.evening_standard.seats` (GROK2).
- **Events** (`Events.check(db, gs) -> Array[Dictionary]`, `StepEvents`): triggered events fire once per game (or per empire for `guardian_of_orn`) on their condition; choice events for the player become a summary decision (`event_choose` with the event instance id and choice id; until answered the event's default — the first choice — is applied at the next turn's end so a pending card never blocks); AI choices by personality (Geese and Pheasants always pick `rivalry`). Every event card shows the cost and the prize before the choice (GDD "bills, not slot machines").
