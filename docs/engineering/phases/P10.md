## Settings, saves, credits, Avipedia, help
- `SettingsScreen`: UI scale 75-200% in steps of 25 (rebuilds the theme live), volumes (master, music, SFX, UI), mute on focus loss, battle watch (watch / skip), Battle Orders (Always / Big / Never), reduce motion, high contrast, summary filters per section. Persisted by `Settings`.
- `SaveLoadScreen`: slots (manual, quicksave, the three autosaves) with turn, race, date; Save / Load / Delete-own-manual-save (confirmation); on web, if `OS.is_userfs_persistent()` is false, a warning line (functional copy) that progress may be lost.
- `CreditsScreen`: built from `credits.json`; the licensed vendor lines appear only when `Sfx.licensed_count() > 0`; `export_web.ps1` writes `build/web/CREDITS.txt` from what is bundled.
- `AvipediaScreen`: tabs Races, Traits, Techs, Parts, Hulls, Buildings, Climates, Events, Rules; each entry renders its name/tip/flavour keys plus numbers from data (no hand-written text); Rules shows the range rule (`ui.battle.range_rule`), retreat receipt, fork rule, Coalition endings, the three Grand Roost routes, using copy keys or GAP fallbacks.
- `HelpOverlay` (H or a "?" button): the hotkey table from `HotkeyTable` with on-screen equivalents.

## Accessibility and visuals
- Minimum 14 px text at 100%; every empire shown by colour **and** glyph; high contrast palette (stronger text/background contrast, thicker lines, no colour-only state).
- VFX pass (GDD §15, no-doughnut rule): stars with diffraction spikes; planets as shaded filled circles; fleets as bird chevrons; Talon tapered strokes, Beak streaks, Horizon darts with trails; sparks and feathers on hits; debris on death; shields as faceted jagged arc segments; explosions as filled starbursts with irregular spikes (`Starburst`), never rings; reduce motion halves particle counts and disables screen shake (there is none by default).
- Screenshot staging (GDD §15): the range strip with band words; Defy card with leader names; capitulation card; Perch Review panel with stars; the audible honk; map climate labels "Ice" and "Jungle" in the system panel.
